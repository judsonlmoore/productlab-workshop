## The behavior change

What will users do differently after this feature ships?

**Sentence structure:**
> [Increase / Decrease] the [number / rate / frequency] of [user behavior X] in order to reach [product metric Y], so that [business outcome Z].

**Your answer:**
Increase the number of venues who send booking requests within 5 minutes of viewing an entertainer profile in order to reach 10 requests per venue per month, so that platform activity increases and both sides see value in the marketplace.

**Example:**
*"Increase the number of users who complete their first data export within 7 days of signup in order to reach a 60% day-30 retention rate, so that monthly churn drops below 6%."*

## The connection to the business outcome

How does this behavior change lead directly to the metric in `business_outcome.md`?

**Your answer (1 sentence):**
When venues can quickly understand what they're paying for and what's included, they move from browsing to requesting faster, which drives the request volume needed to hit the business metric.

---

## What blocks it today

Why isn't this behavior already happening? What is the product limitation standing in the way?

**Your answer (1–2 sentences):**
Venues don't know what pricing includes (duration, equipment, setup) so they hesitate to send requests. Entertainers list broad price ranges without context, leaving venues confused about what they'll actually get for their budget.
